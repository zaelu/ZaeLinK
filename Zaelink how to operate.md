# HOW THE ZAELINK GUNSTOCK IS OPERATED


**Once the gunstock is assembled adjusted and secured and the vr touch controllers inserted in their cups or rings connectors the Zaelink Gunstock is ready to be used.**

**There are several operations that can and are done with the Zaelink VR Gunstock:**

**1. Connecting the gunstock parts to assemble it in its final form while playing a VR shooter game:**  
- First step is to grab the game weapon (rifle shotgun, machinegun, etc) with main hand usually by grabing the pistol like grip of the weapo with the grip button of the controller.

- Second step is to grab the game weapon with the off hand similary using the grip on its off hand controller.

- third step is to let the connection happen cvasi automatically. Because the two halves of the Magnetic Core are now “dangling” at the end of connecting rods in close proximity to each other they will naturaly have the tendency to align and snap to each other. In some ocations, exercised and memorized short movements of the user can bring them closer to an optimal position that facilitates the connection.
 
Once the connection is realized the user can continue to play as the gunstock was of one solid piece.

**2. Disconnecting the gunstock’s two parts.** This is done depending on the Magnetic Core configuration (Fragnetto or Olympic) by slightly rotating the core halves parts one in opposition to the other and then twisting them slightly so they come appart and disconnect then the two hands can be separated to increase distance and thus ensure complete disconnection and afterwards the user can do other operations required by the game mechanics like reloading the gun operating attachments and other machinery, etc. In the case of Olympic mode as the two parts are rotated further they get to the point where they practically start repelling each other and a disconnection is very easy to do and continues just like in the first case (Fragnetto) which was previously described. 

**3. Reloading Pump action Shotguns.** Operating such weapons can be interesting and challanging in VR. The Zaelink VR Gunstock allows the user to reload such weapons while the magnetic connection of the two halves of the Magnetic Core is ON thus making it way easier and more precise to play with such weapons!  
Two ways are available: 

*  The first way is by rotation of the two halves of the core in a way that makes the phisical distance between the two controllers to shorten (usually by bringing the front controller “arm” upwards with such a rotation and usually using one step of 60° of rotation but more might be required in some games or circumstances as it depends on how long tose arms are configured by the user. Sometimes pressing the second off hand controller trigger might be required to initiate the command for ingame reloading. Once the rotation has achieved its desired and necesarry travel distance the user rotates back into initial position the two halves of the core completing the reloading. Note that manual reloading of cartriges in the ammo tube in such weapon is done by normal disconnection of the two halves and normal game operation of the cartriges by the required game mechanic as it’s designed.

*  The second way is by longitudinal movements available when using the extendable connecting rods (usually in Olympic configuration) and by unlocking their exention capability by loosening their two respective  locking nut pairs allowing them free travel. Now the whole gunstock length can vary allowing for realistic longitudinal movement of the reloading lever handle. The reloading thus is done by varying the length of the gunstock. Note tat in such condition the rotating type of reloading operation might still be possible.

**4. Attaching a Cheekrest**  a cheekrest comes usually with the Zaelink VR Gunstock that can be mounted on the top part of the back vertical shoulder resting part. Inside it as also in the end of the butstock top shoulder part magnets can be placed that will add to the overal connection between the Buttstock and the Cheekrest.  

**5. Attaching the Cheekrest Extension** An extension can be added on the top part of the Cheekrest that can provide a quick way of moving laterally in a predetermined way and measure the point of contact between Cheekrest and user face without necesity of readjusting the adjusting ball socket joints or extendable rods.

**6. Adjusting the Gunstock**
Adjusting is done in multiple ways:
 * If the Gunstock is fitted with the "Fragnetto" style connectors that means a "fixed" length o the connecting rods was chosen. Either for all 3 or for some of them. These fixed Connecting Rods need then to be calculated before printing to fit the user or different multiple lengths of them have to be printed. Note that the Locking Nuts that are mounted on them will be locked on to that specific Connecting Rod once glued!  In such case the overall length adjustments is done before assembly at least for that specific segment a fix connector was chosen. Although the connectors are fixed in such case they still can provide little length adjustment by moving them in the ball-socket - this provide a small way to shorten the size and never to increase.
 * If the Gunstock is fitted with "Olympic" style connectors that means their lengths can be controlled much easier and can vary depending on initial size chosen from 8-10cm to around 16cm. These *Connecting Rods* have besides the *Locking Nuts* that lock them on the *Core* or *Cups* or *Buttstock* assemblies *Tightening Nuts* that pass through the middle of them and serve to allow them to be locked to one specific length or move freely like pistons allowing for reloading Pump Action Shotguns by traslational movement rather that rotational one.
 * Besides the length, the *Adjustable Connecting Rods* can swing up and down or left and right few degrees inside their Ball Socket joints. These joints are designed in such way that when fitted with a small patch of double sided thin (and cheap) duck tape (scotch) placed directly on the ball before putting it in the socket and locked, they act like they have a clutch system that present a stiction threshold that can be adjusted by tightening more or less the **Locking Knob** in such way that this articulation practically never moves while the user operates the **Magnetic Core** halves but starts to move with a little bit more force in order to allow user adjustments of its position without any other need of unlocking knobs or screws or safety pins or anything else. Basically on the fly adjustment. When adjusting these rods should be held with the hands as close as possible to the articulation in order to reduce the tension placed on the length of the rods and reduce risk of breaking them when adjusting.
 * **Same Ball Socket Joints** allow to adjust the position of the **Controller Cups** assemblies allowing them to be twiste left and right or up and down to better fit the user ergonomic preferences.
 * **The Buttstock** can be detachable or non detachable or even missing completely as even an option. If present it can be swing up and down left and right by same **Ball Socket Joint** it uses to connect to the rear **Controller Cup** assembly, also it can have its **Connecting Rod** rotated allowing it to start its adjustments by the joint higher or lower, more to the left or more to the right.
 * On Top of the **Buttstock** rear part a **Cheekrest** can be attached. This cheekrest can be preadjusted in length before 3D printing or be raised and lowered with the hole Buttstock.
 * Finally the **Cheekrest** can be fitted with a Cheekrest extension if needed for ergonomy reasons in the case of some virtual weapons. This extension is printable in different sizes.
